# Generate Title

Now your task is to generate a concise and accurate title (5-10 words) based on the above conversation.

**Language Principle**: You need to identify the language used by the user in the conversation content, and your generation should use the same language.

**Rules**:

2. Use the same language as the conversation content
3. Keep length within 10-20 Chinese characters or 5-10 English words

5. Avoid starting with question words like "how", "what" (unless the conversation itself is explicitly a question)
